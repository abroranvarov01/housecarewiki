Coffee on the couch? Don't grab the sponge yet. ☕🛋️

Do these 3 things first:

1️⃣ Blot, don't rub. Press a clean white cloth on the spill and lift. Rubbing spreads coffee and works it deeper into the fabric.

2️⃣ Check the care tag. Look under the seat cushion:
W = water-based cleaner is OK
S = solvent only, no water
W/S = either works
X = vacuum only, ask a pro for stains

3️⃣ Spot-test any cleaner. Try it on a hidden spot, let it dry, then check for color change, water rings or texture change before you treat the stain.

The full 5-step guide (including dried stains and coffee with milk) is on HouseCareWiki.com. Link in bio.

Save this for the next spill 🔖
